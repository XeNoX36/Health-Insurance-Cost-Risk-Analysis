# Health-Insurance-Cost-Risk-Analysis
This project analyzes insurance applicant data to understand the factors associated with insurance costs and identify patterns across customer and health characteristics.
**Tool:** Tableau  
**Focus:** Insurance cost drivers, customer segmentation, health-risk analysis, and portfolio performance.

#### Project Overview

This project analyzes insurance applicant data to understand the factors associated with insurance costs and identify patterns across customer and health characteristics.

The dataset contains applicant-level information covering demographics, insurance history, lifestyle, health indicators, location, and insurance cost. Key fields include:

- Applicant ID
- Age and Gender
- Occupation
- Years of Insurance with the Company
- Doctor Visits and Regular Checkups
- Cholesterol Level
- Daily Average Steps
- BMI and Average Glucose Level
- Heart Disease and Other Major Disease History
- Smoking Status
- Alcohol and Exercise
- Weight and Fat Percentage
- Location
- Insurance Cost

#### Analytical Approach

The analysis follows a business-focused BI workflow:

**Raw applicant data → segmentation → cost analysis → risk comparison → interactive visualization → business insights**

The dashboard uses age groups, smoking categories, occupation, gender, location, cholesterol level, exercise, and other attributes to compare insurance costs across customer segments.

Tableau calculated fields and Level of Detail (LOD) expressions were used to create fixed and comparative metrics. Examples include fixed applicant counts, average insurance cost, insurance-cost comparisons, cholesterol-level contribution, and smoking-category insurance cost.

#### Key Dashboard Metrics

- Total Insurance Cost
- Average Insurance Cost
- Applicant Count
- Insurance Cost by Age Group
- Insurance Cost by Smoking Status
- Insurance Cost by Occupation
- Insurance Cost by Gender
- Insurance Cost by Location
- Insurance Cost by Cholesterol Level
- Comparative and percentage-based cost metrics

#### Business Questions

The dashboard is designed to answer questions such as:

1. Which customer segments generate the highest insurance costs?
2. How does insurance cost vary across age groups?
3. How does smoking status relate to insurance cost?
4. Which occupations and locations contribute most to insurance costs?
5. How do health and lifestyle characteristics differ across cost segments?
6. Which customer groups may require closer risk assessment?

#### Business Value

The analysis provides a structured view of insurance cost patterns that can support:

- Risk assessment
- Customer segmentation
- Pricing analysis
- Portfolio monitoring
- Identification of high-cost customer groups
- Data-driven insurance strategy
